Step Sequencer Every sound is synthesized, and the song is saved in the link.

How it works

There are no recordings here. The kick is a sine wave that falls in pitch, the snare, clap and hat are filtered noise, and the bass and lead are sawtooth waves through a filter. The Tone slider changes something different for each voice: the kick's pitch, the snare's and clap's brightness, how long the hat rings, and how open the synth filters are.

The song is about 40 bytes, packed bit by bit and written into the address after the #. Each change adds a step to the browser's history, so Back undoes it and Forward redoes it. A link to the song is the whole save file: anyone who opens it hears the same thing.

On the Bass and Lead tabs the notes follow the key and scale, so changing the scale changes the melody to fit it.
