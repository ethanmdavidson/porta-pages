SPRAWL

You are a virus: a shape made of capabilities, living in a machine's memory. Copy yourself across the network and crack the Vault.

Each turn

  1. Grow from what the machine holds, and probe to find machines. Both cost cycles
  2. End the turn. The machine's alert rises. If it fills, you're wiped

Moving on

  1. Tap a green machine on the map and plan where you'll land
  2. Open the link. Each end of turn it copies one cell per Leap
  3. Jump when enough of you is across. Whatever isn't is lost
