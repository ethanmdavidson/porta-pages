Girih

The five tiles of Persian strapwork. Lines leave every edge at 54°, so wherever two tiles meet edge to edge the pattern runs on unbroken.

The tiles are scaffolding: when a panel is judged they dissolve and only the strapwork remains. A flawless panel is painted with flowers. Fill the panel outward from the central star. Pick one of the three tiles in your tray, tap a lit edge to try it there, tap the same edge again to turn it, then press Set. A new tile takes its place in the tray. The panel is judged when nothing more will fit. The last few gaps cost the most: a flawless panel earns twice what a 99% one does.

A new game replaces your saved game. Press it again to confirm.
