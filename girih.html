Girih

The five tiles of Persian strapwork. Lines leave every edge at 54°, so wherever two tiles meet edge to edge the pattern runs on unbroken.

The tiles are scaffolding: when a panel is judged they dissolve and only the strapwork remains. A flawless panel is painted with flowers. Fill the panel outward from the central star. Tap a lit edge to try the tile there, tap the same edge again to turn it, then press Set. The panel is judged when nothing more will fit.
