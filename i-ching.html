The two differ in their odds. Coins make changing yin and changing yang lines equally likely. Yarrow stalks make changing yang lines three times as likely as changing yin ones.

Enter your own lines, or look up a hexagram

Six digits, each from 6 to 9, such as 789687. For each line toss three coins, count heads as 3 and tails as 2, and add them up. 6 is old yin (changing), 7 is young yang, 8 is young yin, and 9 is old yang (changing).

Chinese: the received text, from 周易 at Chinese Wikisource. English: James Legge, The Yî King (Sacred Books of the East, vol. XVI, 1882), from English Wikisource.
