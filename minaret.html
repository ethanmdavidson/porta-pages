Minaret

The five girih tiles, laid round a minaret. The band wraps all the way round the tower, so a tile that runs off one side comes back on the other, and the pattern has to meet itself round the back.

Build from the floor up. The view follows the lowest gap still open, and you can lay tiles anywhere below the scaffold line above it. A gap no tile can ever fill, marked red, holds the tower down: when nothing more fits, the tower's height is how far up it is complete.

A small ring marks a joint that can only close with a 108° corner, and only pentagons and rhombuses have one: those corners always meet in pairs. Drag sideways to turn the tower.

A new tower replaces your saved one. Press it again to confirm.
